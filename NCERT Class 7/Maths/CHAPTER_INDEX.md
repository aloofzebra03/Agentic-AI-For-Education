# Class 7 mathematics books

`Ganita_Prakash/Part_1` and `Ganita_Prakash/Part_2` preserve the two books' printed chapter numbering. Each part starts at `S1`; use the book and part together with the chapter number to identify a chapter uniquely. For example: `Ganita_Prakash/Part_1/S1` and `Ganita_Prakash/Part_2/S1` are different chapters.

Chapter filenames contain the `S` number and the title printed in the textbook. Front matter and cover files are not numbered as chapters. Future books should receive a separate book folder, with parts below it where applicable.
